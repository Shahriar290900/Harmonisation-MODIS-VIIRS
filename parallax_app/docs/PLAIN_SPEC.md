Binding: CLAUDE.md, docs/REVAMP_SPEC.md, docs/UI_DESIGN_BRIEF.md. ## The manual (PDF)
- Source `docs/manual/manual.html` generated by `scripts/manual/build_manual.mjs` from the real snapshot (`apps/web/public/data/<default snapshot>/manifest.json, metrics.json, sources.json, regions.json`) and from `apps/web/src/shell/glossary.ts`. Output `apps/web/public/Parallax-Fire-Manual.pdf` (A4, print stylesheet, light theme, readable 11 pt, page numbers, title page, table of contents), rendered with Playwright `page.pdf()` using headless Brave (`/Applications/Brave Browser.app/Contents/MacOS/Brave Browser`; launcher pattern in `scripts/goals/_brave.mjs`).
- Chapters: 1 What Parallax Fire is (one page, plain); 2 Quick start (each page in two lines: Home, Story, Calendar, Map, Two satellites, Live, Results, Data); 3 Reading the calendar (colours, symbols, missing ≠ zero, partial months, unusually high/low); 4 Two satellites and the 2012 jump; 5 How the estimate is made (years learned / chosen / checked, the methods compared, the likely range) — move the current "How it works" page text here; 6 Results by place (table generated from `metrics.json` → `release.regions` and `release.reported_separately`: average miss of ours vs the two simple rules, likely-range coverage, months checked, result); 7 Live data and the Earth view (sources, refresh, what "near-real-time" means, Suomi NPP's empty feed); 8 Data, sources and licences (NASA FIRMS, GIBS, CelesTrak, OpenFreeMap/OpenStreetMap, Natural Earth, geoBoundaries) and the FIRMS acknowledgement; 9 Limits and honest use; 10 Glossary (plain word → technical term); 11 Credits: Parallax Fire by Team Lumos, created by M Al Shahriar Hameem; team members; NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 — Harmonization of MODIS and VIIRS Hot Spots.
- Every number is generated from the files (no typed numbers). Re-runnable: `node scripts/manual/build_manual.mjs`.

## The plain app
- Every app page: `<h1>` + at most one short line (≤ 15 words) + the controls and visuals. No explanatory paragraphs; detail goes to the manual. Honesty stays, but as short labels and tooltips ("estimate", "no data", "not a confirmed wildfire").
- "How it works" becomes **Results** (route id stays `method`): the per-place table (plain headers) + one line + "Full method in the manual (PDF)".
- Data & sources: sources table, downloads (CSV, brief), snapshot facts; one line; licences → manual.
- Calendar month summary (Evidence): two short lines max (measured / estimate with its likely range in words / status); the rest behind a closed "More" disclosure.
- Two satellites: switch + chart + one changing sentence; remove other prose.
- Story page: keep, but each step ≤ 45 words.
- Home (landing): keep the scroll story, but cut each chapter's text to ≤ 35 words; no paragraph walls.
- A "Manual" control `[data-manual-link]` (book icon + "Manual") in the top bar on every app page and in the Home nav, opening the PDF in a new tab.
