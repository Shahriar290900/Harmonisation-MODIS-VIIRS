# S063 - GFED data page (globalfiredata.org/data.html)
Fetched 2026-10-10 (HTTP 200; relayed by summarising model; page gives little detail).

VERIFIED FACTS
1. GFED5 / GFED5.1: gridded NetCDF4 burned area and emissions. Page says to prefer GFED5 over GFED4s "when possible".
2. GFED5.1 covers 1997-2022; GFED5.1NRT from 2023 onward (reprocessed and update directories).
3. Page does not state GFED5 grid resolution; it states GFED spatial resolution 0.25 deg and that GFED5 is not provided at 500 m.
4. GFED5 includes small-fire burned area. Inputs for GFED5 not listed on the page (satellite data "mostly 500 m"; MODIS used for GFED4s).
5. Download is via SFTP on port 1022 (login details in a linked text file). Licence and GFED5 citation: not stated on the page.

USE IN PROJECT: Coarse (0.25 deg) long-run country-scale context only; too coarse for AOI calendar validation and SFTP access makes it a poor fit for a web-app pipeline. Treat licence/citation as UNVERIFIED until read from the GFED5 paper.
