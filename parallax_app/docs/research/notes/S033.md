## Verified facts (seen 2026-10-10; quotes from the page via the fetch tool)
1. Confidence: "Confidence values are set to low (l), nominal (n), and high (h)." Low = daytime pixels "typically associated with areas of Sun glint"; nominal = free of Sun glint in daytime and a "strong (>15 K) temperature anomaly"; high = "associated with day or nighttime saturated pixels".
2. Pixel size: "The algorithm produces approximately 375 m pixels at nadir." "Scan and track reflect actual pixel size." The page does NOT describe growth with scan angle.
3. `type` (inferred hot spot type): 0 presumed vegetation fire; 1 active volcano; 2 other static land source; 3 offshore (includes all detections over water). KEY: the page says the attribute is "only available for VNP14IMGT (standard quality) data." So NRT rows (VNP14IMGTDL_NRT) may have no `type` value. Null `type` rule is needed in CONTRACT.
4. `version`: "identifies the collection (e.g., VIIRS Collection 1 or VIIRS Collection 2), and source of data processing". Examples: "2.0URT", "2.0RT", "1.0NRT", "1.0" (standard = collection number alone). The page shows no "2.0NRT" example, so how a 2.0 NRT row looks is not confirmed here.
5. `satellite` codes: "N = Suomi NPP; N20 = NOAA-20 (JPSS1); N21 = NOAA-21 (JPSS2)". CONTRACT maps NOAA-20 to code `1`; this page says `N20`. (Check the actual CSV values: SOURCE_CHECK saw `N` for S-NPP in Bangladesh files.) The numeric `1` in CONTRACT may be an internal encoding; flag for the owner.
6. `daynight`: "D= Daytime fire, N= Nighttime fire". `frp`: "pixel-integrated fire radiative power in megawatts (MW)"; sub-pixel FRP retrieval "only viable across small and/or low-intensity fires". `acq_time`: "Time of acquisition/overpass of the satellite (in UTC)."
7. Coverage dates shown: S-NPP "20 January 2012 - present"; NOAA-20 "1 January 2020 - present"; NOAA-21 "17 January 2024 - present". The Download page (S037) says NOAA-20 from "1 April 2018". The pack's observation is correct: the two FIRMS pages disagree; neither explains why.
