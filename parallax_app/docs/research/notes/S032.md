## Verified facts (seen 2026-10-10; quotes via the fetch tool)
1. Coverage: Aqua "3 July 2002 - present"; Terra "1 November 2000 - present". So the pack's Aqua date (03 Jul 2002) matches this page; our docs' 2002-07-04 does not (one day off; no consequence, 2002 is `unsupported`, but align provenance text to 2002-07-03).
2. Latency: "within 3 hours for global data" and "within 1-30 minutes for ultra and real-time in the US and Canada."
3. `scan`/`track`: "The algorithm produces 1 km fire pixels, but MODIS pixels get bigger toward the edge of scan." They "reflect actual pixel size." No growth numbers given.
4. `satellite`: "A = Aqua and T = Terra."
5. `confidence`: "Confidence estimates range between 0 and 100%", "based on a collection of intermediate algorithm quantities"; pixels are assigned one of three classes (low, nominal, high). NO numeric thresholds for the classes are on the page (the familiar 0-29/30-79/80-100 cut points are UNVERIFIED from this page; from memory only).
6. `version`: "6.1URT", "6.1RT", "6.1NRT", "6.1" (Standard).
7. `type`: 0 presumed vegetation fire, 1 active volcano, 2 other static land source, 3 offshore. "This attribute is only available for MCD14DL (standard quality) data." So MODIS NRT rows have no meaningful `type`.
8. Orbit context: Terra "passes from north to south across the equator in the morning"; Aqua "passes south to north over the equator in the afternoon." Exact local times are not on the page (the ~10:30 / ~13:30 figures are UNVERIFIED here).
9. `frp`: "pixel-integrated fire radiative power in MW"; `lat/lon`: "Center of 1 km fire pixel, but not necessarily the actual location of the fire."; `daynight` D/N.
