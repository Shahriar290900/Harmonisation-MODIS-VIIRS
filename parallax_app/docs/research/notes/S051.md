# S051 HF dataset card: jq5522/aml_indo_fires (Indonesian Wildfire Panel 2012-2025)
Fetched 2026-10-10 by R4 via WebFetch (HTTP 200, summariser output). Nothing downloaded.

## Verified facts (card as summarised)
1. Licence CC BY 4.0 (ERA5 parts under Copernicus licence). Size 581 MB total, 44,112 rows in the core panel.
2. Grid: 0.25 x 0.25 deg aligned to ERA5, box 94.0E-119.5E, 6.5S-8.5N (Sumatra, Kalimantan); ~1,839 land cells. Time: January 2012 - December 2025 (168 months), cell-month panel.
3. Fire source: "NASA FIRMS VIIRS SNPP" only, 2012-2025. The card says nothing about MODIS or harmonization (so it is NOT a cross-sensor precedent). Precedent that S-NPP-only 2012-2025 monthly panels exist.
4. Columns (core): cell_id, year, month, time, lon_centre, lat_centre, fire_count_lnh, fire_count_nh, fire_any_nh, frp_sum_nh, t2m, tp, u10, v10, wind_speed, peat_fraction, oil_palm_fraction, road_density_km_per_km2. `_lnh` = low+nominal+high, `_nh` = nominal+high confidence only (card shows both series; models use `_nh`).
5. "Months with no fire detections are filled with zeros, not dropped." Zero-fill treats no-detection as zero; our app keeps no-data distinct from zero (pack p9) - note the difference.
6. Splits: 2012-2013 burn-in; 2014-2021 train (expanding window); 2022-2023 outer validation; 2024-2025 final test "touched once, at reporting"; inner CV 4 expanding folds (val years 2018-2021). Positive rate ~33%. Similar discipline to ours (train 2013-2020, val 2021-2022, test 2023-2024).
7. Minor: citation key `aml_indo_fires_2025` but year listed 2026.

USE IN PROJECT: schema template only (cell-month panel with `_lnh`/`_nh` confidence variants - supports an explicit confidence-filter sensitivity column in our panel). The "test touched once" wording is a good practice to mirror, in our own words. Tropical Indonesia; not Bangladesh ground truth.
