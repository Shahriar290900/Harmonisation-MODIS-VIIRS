# S055 - Giglio, Schroeder, Justice 2016, "The collection 6 MODIS active fire detection algorithm and fire products"
Fetched 2026-10-10 from https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6110110/ (HTTP 200, open-access author manuscript). Content was returned via WebFetch's summarising model, so quotes are as relayed; re-check exact wording before putting them in a slide.

WHAT IT IS: Remote Sensing of Environment 178:31-41 (2016), DOI 10.1016/j.rse.2016.02.054, authors Louis Giglio, Wilfrid Schroeder, Christopher O. Justice. This is the Collection 6 MODIS (MOD14/MYD14) algorithm paper, NOT a MODIS-vs-VIIRS comparison.

VERIFIED FACTS
1. Overall omission error vs ASTER-detected fires: 86.2%; below 10% for fires of >=140 ASTER pixels; "stabilizes at ~5%" above ~250 ASTER fire pixels (ASTER fire pixel is 30 m).
2. Regional detection probability ranged 7% (Middle East) to 26% (Australia and New Zealand); variability "strongly driven by regional differences in fire size". => detection differs by region, so one global MODIS-to-VIIRS multiplier is unsafe.
3. Daytime global commission error 2.4% (C5) -> 1.2% (C6); no evidence of commission errors in sampled night data (838 night MOD14 pixels of ~500,000 sampled). False alarms mainly a daytime problem.
4. Confidence is a heuristic: geometric mean of up to five sub-confidences (C1-C5), each a 0-1 ramp. It is not a calibrated probability.
5. Algorithm works at 1 km, 1354 samples per scan line; MODIS pixel area "varies as a function of scan angle" (no per-angle values given).
6. Validation reference: 2466 ASTER scenes, ~640 grid cells (140 at night); ASTER minimum detectable fire ~4 m2 for a 900 K flaming fire. Validation is Terra MODIS only; Aqua described, not validated.
7. C6 algorithm is the baseline for the S-NPP VIIRS 750 m product (Csiszar et al. 2014). No direct MODIS-VIIRS comparison in this paper.
8. Paper does not describe the FIRMS CSV `type` field (UNVERIFIED elsewhere; look at FIRMS docs, group A).

CAVEATS: Terra only validated; omission 86.2% is relative to ASTER-detectable fires, dominated by small fires.

USE IN PROJECT: 
- Cite for "MODIS misses most small fires; detection probability depends on fire size and region" (motivates why VIIRS 375 m counts jump and why calibration must be region/land-cover aware).
- Cite 'confidence' as a heuristic, so do not treat MODIS 0-100 and VIIRS low/nominal/high as comparable probabilities.
- Do not cite for any VIIRS claim.
