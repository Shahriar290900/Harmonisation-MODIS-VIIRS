VERIFIED FACTS
1. Authors: Niels Andela, Douglas C. Morton, Louis Giglio, Ronan Paugam, Yang Chen, Stijn Hantson, Guido R. van der Werf, James T. Randerson. Published 24 Apr 2019.
2. Quote: "The algorithm identified 13.3 million individual fires over the study period." Quote: "multiday fires contributed the majority of burned area in all biomass burning regions."
3. Input: "daily moderate-resolution (500 m) Collection 6 MCD64A1 burned-area data". Validation used VIIRS active-fire detections, MTBS perimeters (30 m Landsat) and NIROPS perimeters for 15 fires.
4. Period 2003-2016 (individual-fire data begin 2003 when Terra+Aqua combined improved burn-date certainty). Minimum detected fire: one MODIS pixel (~21 ha, as quoted by the paper).
5. Caveats quoted: moderate-resolution BA products "are unable to adequately map the occurrence of small fires" (cropland burning underestimated); burn-date uncertainty "typically at least 1 d"; "sub-daily fire dynamics cannot be resolved"; durations underestimated in forests (smouldering); speed/direction not directly validated.
6. Dataset DOI 10.3334/ORNLDAAC/1642; article CC BY 4.0 (the article licence; the dataset's own terms were not read here).

USE IN PROJECT: Only an event-level context benchmark for 2003-2016 (ends before VIIRS S-NPP is long enough, so it overlaps only 2012-2016). Derived from MCD64A1, so not independent of MODIS. Not suitable for Bangladesh cropland small-fire validation (small-fire caveat).
