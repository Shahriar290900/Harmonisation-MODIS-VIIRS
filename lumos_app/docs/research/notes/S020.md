# S020 - Earthdata catalog page: VNP64A1 V002 (VIIRS/NPP Burned Area Monthly L4 Global 500m)
Fetched 2026-10-10 by R3 from https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/catalog/lpcloud-vnp64a1-002 (reached via the doi.org redirect noted earlier). Text relayed by WebFetch's summarising model, so quotes are as relayed.

VERIFIED FACTS
1. Title "VIIRS/NPP Burned Area Monthly L4 Global 500m SIN Grid V002", short name VNP64A1, version 002. DOI https://doi.org/10.5067/VIIRS/VNP64A1.002.
2. Temporal coverage on this page: "2012-03-01 to Present", Data State ACTIVE. (The Earth Engine card S012 says 2012-01-19; the two differ, quote each with its source.)
3. KEY: "The delivery of all Suomi National Polar-orbiting Partnership (Suomi NPP) VIIRS science data products will cease on November 2, 2026 (2026/306)." (The pack says 1 Nov; this page says 2 Nov 2026, day 306.)
4. The page tells users: "Data users should transition now to alternative products from NOAA-21 and NOAA-20." It does NOT say VNP64A1 itself continues, and names no VNP64A1 successor product.
5. Algorithm: "The VNP64 burned area mapping approach employs 750 m VIIRS imagery coupled with 750 m VIIRS active fire observations." Hybrid: dynamic thresholds on a burn-sensitive SWIR vegetation index plus a temporal texture measure; burn date to the nearest day per 500 m cell. 500 m, monthly.
6. V2 changes listed: improved calibration for the whole S-NPP mission, better geolocation, aerosol-flag fix over bright surfaces, improved cloud mask, MODIS-heritage 7-class land/water mask.

ANSWER to R5 question (does VNP64A1 continue after S-NPP ends?): NOT STATED. Treat VNP64A1 as an S-NPP-only record ending around 2 Nov 2026. Do not plan on it for validation after that; it is only a historical VIIRS-era check.
CAVEATS: input is the 750 m VIIRS active fire product, not the 375 m VNP14IMG that Lumos uses, but burned-area maps still depend on VIIRS fire detections (not independent truth).
