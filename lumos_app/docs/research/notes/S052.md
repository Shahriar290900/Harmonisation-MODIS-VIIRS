# S052 HF dataset card: links-ads/hotspot-dataset (LINKS hotspot disambiguation)
Fetched 2026-10-10 by R4 via WebFetch (HTTP 200; summariser output of the card). Nothing downloaded.

## Verified facts (card as summarised)
1. Licence: cc-by-4.0. Size: 2.23 GB total. The card gives no total row count; `dataset_500.csv` is "about 500k data points" and is the stratified subset (by spatial distribution, 10% positive / 90% negative) used for the paper's results; full `dataset.csv` is unsized.
2. Viewer schema, 20 columns: id, is_positive, frp, firepixel, timestamp, t_21, t_31, confidence, inferred_type, resolution, t_m13, t_m15, t_i4, t_i5, lat, lon, daynight, week_sin, week_cos, is_vegetation_fire. Card does not define the label columns (is_positive presumed target, not stated).
3. Source: "MODIS/VIIRS hotspots" intersected in time and space with EFFIS burned areas ("temporal and spatial data intersection") to label vegetation-fire vs other.
4. Coverage not stated in the card; the paper title says Europe and preview coordinates are mostly Europe. Years not stated (100-row preview starts at 2002).
5. Citation: arXiv 2308.02508, IGARSS 2023, DOI 10.57967/hf/2048 (the pack's Urbanelli 2023 link is the same arXiv id, consistent with the group-B mapping).
6. Viewer is broken for this dataset ("DatasetGenerationError ... Float value 313.2 was truncated converting to int64").
7. Columns include `inferred_type` and `is_vegetation_fire`, i.e. a learned notion of non-vegetation hotspots, parallel to FIRMS `type`.

## Caveats
- Labels depend on EFFIS burned-area polygons (Europe). Pack says: "Do not treat Europe-trained labels as Bangladesh ground truth". Agreed; also not California/NSW.
- Sensor mixture and years unknown; cannot be used as a calibration set.

## VERDICT: AVOID for the product (optional reading only). 2.23 GB, Europe-only labels, no use for the cross-sensor count calibration. Citing the arXiv paper as background for "hotspot is not wildfire" is fine; do not ingest the data.
