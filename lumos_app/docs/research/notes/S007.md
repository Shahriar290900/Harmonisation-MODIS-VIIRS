# S007 - Earth Engine catalog: MCD64A1.061 MODIS Burned Area Monthly Global 500m
Fetched 2026-10-10 from https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/MODIS_061_MCD64A1 (HTTP 200; text relayed by WebFetch's summarising model).

VERIFIED FACTS
1. EE ID `MODIS/061/MCD64A1`; V6.1, Terra and Aqua combined; 500 m; monthly; provider NASA LP DAAC at USGS EROS.
2. Availability: metadata lists 2000-11-01 to 2026-07-01, but the page summary text says 2000-11-01 to 2025-08-01. Page itself is inconsistent; check with the live ImageCollection (end date) before claiming "to present".
3. Bands: `BurnDate` (0-366; 0 unburned, 1-366 approx Julian day of burning), `Uncertainty` (0-100, estimated uncertainty in burn day), `QA` (bitmask: bit0 land/water, bit1 valid data, bit2 shortened mapping period, bit3 relabeled in contextual phase, bits5-7 special condition code for unburned cells), `FirstDay`, `LastDay` (first/last day of year of reliable change detection).
4. Description: "combines 500m MODIS Surface Reflectance imagery with 1km MODIS active fire observations". So the product uses MODIS active fires as an input: it is NOT independent truth for MODIS hot spots (consistent with pack p6).
5. Licence: "MODIS data and products acquired through the LP DAAC have no restrictions on subsequent use, sale, or redistribution." Cite via LP DAAC "Citing Our Data". DOI https://doi.org/10.5067/MODIS/MCD64A1.061.

CAVEATS: BurnDate is day of year within the monthly image; join to month via image date. Dependence on MODIS active fire makes it circular for MODIS-side validation; for VIIRS-vs-MODIS consistency it is still useful as a MODIS-era secondary check.

USE IN PROJECT: Use as secondary seasonal/timing consistency layer only; label "not independent" in the Sensor Lab. Provenance row: MODIS/061/MCD64A1, V6.1, DOI above.
