VERIFIED FACTS
1. EE ID `NASA/VIIRS/002/VNP64A1`; V2 (002); 500 m; monthly; global; provider NASA LP DAAC at USGS EROS.
2. Availability on page: 2012-01-19 to 2026-08-01 (UTC). Start 19 Jan 2012 matches the S-NPP era (pack says Jan 2012).
3. Derived from daily Suomi NPP VIIRS burned area data. Quote: "The VNP64 burned area mapping approach employs 750m VIIRS imagery coupled with 750m VIIRS active fire observations." Page names Suomi NPP only, no NOAA-20.
   => It uses the 750 m VIIRS active-fire product, not the 375 m FIRMS VNP14IMG that Lumos uses. Dependence is partial/indirect but it is still not independent of VIIRS fire detection.
4. Bands: `Burn_Date` (1-366), `Burn_Date_Uncertainty` (days), `QA` (same bit layout as MCD64A1), `First_Day`, `Last_Day`. Note band names differ from MCD64A1 (underscores; `Burn_Date` vs `BurnDate`).
5. Terms: freely accessible, cite the dataset; no named licence. DOI https://doi.org/10.5067/VIIRS/VNP64A1.002. Citation via LP DAAC "Citing Our Data".
