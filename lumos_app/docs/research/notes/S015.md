# S015 - MCD64A1 (Giglio 2018) via Earthdata catalog page and LP DAAC User Guide V6.1
The paper itself (doi 10.1016/j.rse.2018.08.005) was NOT read (Elsevier blocked earlier; metadata only). This note is from two NASA/USGS pages: https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/catalog/lpcloud-mcd64a1-061 (relayed by WebFetch's summariser) and the User Guide PDF https://lpdaac.usgs.gov/documents/1006/MCD64_User_Guide_V61.pdf (35 pages; downloaded by the fetch tool, ~1 MB; I extracted its text locally with pypdf and read it directly, so the quotes are verbatim from the PDF text, with small spacing glitches from extraction).

VERIFIED FACTS
1. Product: monthly global 500 m, Terra + Aqua, Collection 6.1; 2000-11-01 to Present; DOI 10.5067/MODIS/MCD64A1.061. User guide p8.2: not distributed before November 2000 (Terra calibration).
2. Dependence on active fires (user guide section 2): "The MCD64 burned-area mapping approach employs 500-m MODIS imagery coupled with 1-km MODIS active fire observations." and "Cumulative active fire maps are used to guide the selection of burned and unburned training samples and to guide the specification of prior probabilities." => MCD64A1 is NOT independent of MODIS active fires. Using it to validate MODIS hotspots is circular for detection; it is fairer for VIIRS-vs-burn-scar checks or for event/season totals.
3. Version note: "neither algorithm nor product changes were made for Collection 6.1"; inputs are C6.1 surface reflectance and active fire.
4. Output: burn date as ordinal day 1-366 per 500 m cell, 0 unburned, to the nearest day.
5. Validation (section 7): 558 Landsat 8 OLI image pairs, 1 Mar 2014 to 19 Mar 2015, stratified random in space and time. "Globally, the MCD64A1 product had an estimated 40.2% commission error and 72.6% omission error"; bias of global area burned "-54.1%". At 3, 4, 5, 6 km cells: r2 > 0.70, slope > 0.79, intercept -0.0030; the negative bias is "primarily due to the systematic underestimation of smaller burned areas".
6. Known problems: cropland burning "should generally be treated as low confidence"; June 2001 Terra outage degrades that month; Aqua outage 16 Aug to 2 Sep 2020 (loss of Aqua data over Africa, eastern Asia, Indonesia, Oceania; Terra continued).
7. Validation references named: Boschetti et al. 2016 (RSE 186, 465-478, stratified random sampling design in space and time) and Boschetti et al. 2019 (RSE 235, global validation of C6).
8. ATBD link: https://lpdaac.usgs.gov/documents/115/MCD64_ATBD_V6.pdf (not fetched).
The guide gives no overpass times (searched for 10:30/13:30/equator: none).

CAVEATS: Only a 1 km MODIS fire input is named; the guide does not say MOD14/MYD14 by name in section 2. The first guessed URL (document id 1005) returned 404; the correct id is 1006.
